# MOJAD INTELLIGENCE, RADAR & EARLY-WARNING ARCHITECTURE™ v1.0

Status: DESIGN / FOUNDATION

## Purpose
Understand authorized world signals, detect changes, verify them, identify risks/opportunities early and help humans act.

## Power model
Platform power = Data Access × Coverage × Freshness × Verification × Intelligence × Compute × Actionability × Security.

## World Sensory System
World → Sources → Ingestion → Evidence → Knowledge Graph → Intelligence → Radars → Early Warning → Opportunity Engine → AI → Authorized Action.

## Radars
- Weather/Environment
- Market
- Business
- Technology
- Science
- Economic
- Crypto/Pi
- Country/Government
- Public social signals where lawful and permitted
- Cyber threat

## Signal lifecycle
Normal → small change → repeated change → multi-source confirmation → anomaly → potential event → early warning.

## Intelligence Swarm
World, Climate, Market, Business, Science, Technology, Crypto/Pi, Government, Security, Education, Opportunity, Evidence, Synthesis and Early-Warning agents.

## Master orchestrator
Request → intent → decomposition → agent selection → permissions → parallel work → evidence → conflict analysis → synthesis → confidence → human review where required → response/action.

## Signal fusion
Multiple independent sources can increase confidence. Correlation is not causation.

## Observation vs action
Seeing data does not grant action authority.

## Opportunity-before-everyone workflow
World signal → verification → Knowledge Graph → impact → Opportunity DNA → matching → authorized action.

## Definition of Done
Sources, legal access, ingestion, freshness, evidence, conflict resolution, Knowledge Graph, signal fusion, anomaly detection, radar, early warning, AI agents, human approval, security, privacy, explainability, outcomes, learning and recovery must be designed and tested before production claims.
