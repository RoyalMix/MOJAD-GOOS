## 1. Intake

Record the request date, channel, request type, relevant account identifiers, requested action and a safe contact method. Do not request more identity evidence than necessary. Publish an intake channel only after a responsible owner verifies it: **[REQUIRES VERIFIED INPUT]**.

## 2. Verify and scope

A trained, authorized reviewer should verify the requester using an approved, proportionate method; identify affected systems and processors; clarify scope when necessary; and avoid disclosing data before identity and authority are established. Actual identity verification and case-management tools: **[IMPLEMENTATION NOT VERIFIED]**.

## 3. Assess

Classify the request (for example, access, correction, deletion, export, objection, restriction or consent withdrawal) only as appropriate under applicable law. Identify exceptions, third-party data, legal holds, security/fraud records and effects on service operation. Applicable rights, exemptions and deadlines: **[REQUIRES QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW]**.

## 4. Fulfill and document

Assign a case owner; record systems searched, actions taken, processors contacted, limitations and reviewer approval. Ensure fulfillment does not expose another person’s data or alter authoritative payment, security or audit records without the relevant owner/legal review. ## 5. Respond and close
