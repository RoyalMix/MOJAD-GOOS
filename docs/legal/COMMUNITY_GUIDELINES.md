## 1. Purpose

MOJAD GOOS aims to support respectful participation and credible opportunity discovery. Applicable product scope and community features must be confirmed before publication.

## 2. Expected conduct

Users should provide accurate information, respect other participants’ privacy and rights, communicate lawfully and avoid misleading opportunity, business, employment, investment, education or service claims. No listing or profile is verified merely because it appears on the platform.

## 3. Prohibited conduct

Subject to counsel and final policy approval, the platform may prohibit:

- Fraud, scams, impersonation, forged evidence and deceptive payment requests.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, exploitation and targeted abuse.
- Unauthorized disclosure of personal, private, confidential or sensitive information.
- Intellectual-property infringement or publication without required rights/consent.
- Spam, manipulation of rankings/trust/reputation, fake engagement or referral abuse.
- Malicious links, malware, attempts to bypass access controls or misuse of AI/tools.
- False claims of Pi endorsement, verified status, guaranteed jobs/funding/returns or unsupported MOJAD capabilities.

Definitions, legal boundaries and reporting obligations require **[REQUIRES QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW]**.

## 4. Opportunity integrity

Listings should identify their source and be kept accurate. Users should report suspected scams, expired or duplicate listings, misleading terms and unsafe requests. Verification labels must describe only what was checked; see the [Pi Integration Standard](../standards/mojad/13_PI_ECOSYSTEM_INTEGRATION_STANDARD.md).

## 5. Reporting and review

Report channels, moderation scope, response times, evidence handling, enforcement decisions and appeals are **[REQUIRES VERIFIED INPUT]**. No response-time or enforcement promise is made by this draft. Any implementation must protect reporters and respect privacy, due process and applicable law.

## 6. AI, payments and safety

AI suggestions are not verified facts. AI must not authorize financial or consequential actions. Payment, reward and trust claims require authoritative evidence; see [AI Standard](../standards/mojad/05_MOJAD_AI_STANDARD.md), [Acceptance Tests](../standards/mojad/08_ACCEPTANCE_TESTS.md), and [Trust Constitution](../../governance/TRUST_CONSTITUTION.md).
